# gr0ve 🌲

**gr0ve** is the ultimate all-in-one companion for the Bergen County Academies (BCA) community. Designed with a premium aesthetic and a focus on seamless utility, it brings everything you need to navigate your school day into a single, beautiful interface.

---

## 🚀 App Breakdown

### 📱 Adaptive Dashboard
The heart of gr0ve. Your home screen automatically adapts its layout and widgets based on the time of day:
- **Morning**: Quick access to bus arrivals and today's schedule.
- **School Hours**: Real-time teacher absences and current period tracking.
- **Evening**: Tomorrow's outlook and lingering notifications.

### 🤖 AI Counselor
Meet Grover, Aspen, Rowan, and the rest of the gang. 
- **Persona-Driven**: Choose from multiple counselor personas, each with unique voices and specialized guidance.
- **Voice Mode**: Fully immersive voice conversations powered by advanced TTS.
- **Hidden Personas**: Discover and unlock secret characters protected by the **Almighty Grove Keeper**.

### 📅 Smart Scheduling
- **BCA Calendar**: Automatically synced with school events, assemblies, and deadlines.
- **Personal Events**: Add your own assignments and reminders directly into the integrated calendar.

### 🚌 Real-Time Logistics
- **Bus Tracking**: View live parking locations for every bus in the BCA fleet.
- **Smart Notifications**: Get notified exactly when your starred buses arrive or depart.

### 🍎 Teacher Absences
- **Live Updates**: Stay informed with up-to-the-minute teacher attendance and class coverage.
- **Admin Excellence**: A reactive, ultra-compact grid system for high-speed management.

### 🍽️ Lunch & Nutrition
- **Daily Menu**: Browse today's offerings with full nutritional breakdowns.
- **Safety First**: Integrated allergen tracking to keep you safe and informed.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) (Multi-platform excellence)
- **Backend**: [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions)
- **UI/UX**: [HugeIcons](https://hugeicons.com/) (Premium iconography), Glassmorphism, and Dynamic Theming.
- **AI**: Ollama & AWS Polly/Coqui for voice and intelligence.
