## 1. OVERVIEW OF BCA SCHEDULING SYSTEM

BCA uses a trimester system. The school year is divided into three trimesters.

**Period structure:**

- Periods 1–8 are the main scheduled periods (full-year courses run here).
- Period 9 is used for trimester electives, sports, theatre productions, and other activities.
- Wednesday is "Projects" day — a special schedule day distinct from M/T/R/F.

**Days notation:**

- M/R = Monday/Thursday (2 days per week)
- T/F = Tuesday/Friday (2 days per week)
- M/R and T/F = 4 days per week
- M/T/R/F = 4 days per week (all non-Wednesday days)

**Unscheduled periods:**

- Rising 10th graders: maximum 7 unscheduled periods per week (not including lunch)
- Rising 11th graders: maximum 8 unscheduled periods per week (not including lunch)
- Students with too many unscheduled periods may be placed into electives to meet the minimum

## 3. SCHEDULING TIMELINE & PROCESS

### For Rising 10th Graders (Sophomores)

- **Feb 11 – March 6**: Students input course requests via Google Form
- **Mid June**: Students receive their scheduled course list
- **Late June**: Students request period 9 projects and electives for Trimester 1; ADD/DROP opportunity
- **July – August**: No schedule changes
- **Early September**: Students may make schedule changes with counselor

### For Rising 11th Graders (Juniors)

- **January**: Rising Junior class scheduling meeting held
- **Jan 29 – March 6**: Students meet with counselors and enter course requests (periods 1–8 only)
- **June**: Students receive course listing; then request period 9 projects and electives for Trimester 1

### For Rising 12th Graders (Seniors)

- **January through early March**: Meet with counselor to discuss and enter course requests
- **June**: BCA emails students their scheduled courses

### General Notes

- 12th graders are scheduled first, then 11th graders, then lower grades
- For each individual student: core classes are scheduled first → 3- and 4-day electives → 2-day-per-week electives
- Your best opportunity to get the courses you want is during the Course Request Process (CRP)
- Counselor meetings are available if students want to discuss options before submitting

---

## 4. HOW THE SCHEDULE IS BUILT

- BCA makes a **best effort** to honor each student's course requests
- Nearly all requests are accommodated, but some exceptions occur due to capacity and scheduling constraints
- Students **cannot request more classes than can possibly fit** in their schedule
- For 11th grade, course requests are for **periods 1–8 only** (period 9 is handled separately in June)
- Alternates: Students may (and often should) submit an alternate choice for electives in case their primary is full or doesn't fit

## 6. RISING 10TH GRADE (SOPHOMORES)

### What Sophomores Choose During CRP

Sophomores make **two choices** for next year:

1. **Math level** (based on teacher recommendation visible in PowerSchool)
2. **Full-year two-day-per-week elective** (encouraged but not required)

Sophomores do NOT select their core history, English, science, or language — those are determined by academy and sequence.

Sophomores also select period 9 elective and project requests separately (in late June for Tri 1, and at the start of each subsequent trimester). See Section 17 for the full trimester elective catalog.

### Art Credit Requirement Note

- Graduation requires **6 art credits**
- 3 credits are fulfilled by the mandatory 9th grade art class
- Sophomores still need **3 more art credits** (= 3 one-trimester courses OR 1 full-year art elective)
- Art-eligible courses are marked in the elective catalogs
- Options for earning art credits: Period 9 electives in off-season, Projects, Lunch Choir, year-long two-day elective

## 7. RISING 11TH GRADE (JUNIORS)

### What Juniors Choose During CRP

Juniors make the most complex set of choices, as many decisions affect 12th grade as well:

1. **Math level**
2. **History level** (may affect IB Diploma eligibility)
3. **World Language level** (SL vs HL — HL is a 2-year commitment)
4. **English option** (IB Lit & Language HL vs IB Literature HL)
5. **Science level** (for some academies — Biology vs IB Biology SL)
6. **AP/IB elective** (one slot available)
7. **Full-year two-day-per-week elective(s)** (one or two slots typically available)

Note: 11th grade requests are for **periods 1–8 only**. Period 9 requests are entered separately in June.

Most rising 11th grade students have space for **one AP/IB elective** and **one or two two-day-per-week electives**.

## 9. IB DIPLOMA PROGRAM

### Overview

The IB Diploma was previously only available to ABF and ACAHA students. **Beginning 2026-27, it is available to all academies.**

### Core Requirements to Earn the IB Diploma

**Requirement 1: Six IB Courses (3 or 4 at Higher Level)**

1. English HL
2. Math HL or SL (12th Grade)
3. History HL or SL
4. World Language: HL or SL (Spanish/French) OR Ab Initio (Mandarin)
5. An IB Science: IB Biology SL, IB Environmental Systems SL, or IB Computer Science SL
6. Another IB course (varies by academy)

**Requirement 2: Theory of Knowledge (TOK)**

- Full-year, two-day-per-week elective
- Must be taken during BOTH 11th and 12th grades
- Can be scheduled periods 1–8 OR at period 9 (to free up a 2-day elective slot)

**Requirement 3: CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service)**

- Students participate in and document CAS activities across both years
- Involves creative pursuits, physical activity, and service to others
- Includes goals, regular reflection, and a CAS project

**Requirement 4: Extended Essay (EE)**

- Independent self-directed research project
- Focused research question in a subject of student's choice
- Culminates in a 4,000-word academic paper
- Completed across two years, supported during project time

### Should I Consider the IB Diploma? — Decision Questions

1. Are you planning to take **4 years of world language**? (If NO → diploma likely not a fit)
2. Are you planning to take an **advanced history course** (HL for 2 years, or SL for 1 year)? (If NO → likely not a fit)
3. As an 11th grader, will you be taking **Calculus**? (If NO → can't satisfy IB Math requirement, unless in ABF or ACAHA who have an extra math slot in 12th grade)

If YES to all three → consider the trade-offs:

- Pursuing the diploma limits elective flexibility
- You may not be able to fit desired courses like AP Physics, AP Art History, Adv Math Topics, Multivariable Calculus, Data Structures, or certain upper-level electives
- Consider your commitments: research, performance, sports, etc.

### HL Requirement Summary

- Must have 3 or 4 courses at Higher Level
- English HL is required for all diploma students
- ABF students already have IB Economics HL in core → only need History or World Language or Math HL additionally
- ACAHA students have IB Business SL in core → need HL in History, World Language, or Math
- AEDT/ATCS/AVPA students: HL typically in History and English, plus one of World Language or Math
- If World Language is Mandarin (Ab Initio only, not offered at HL) → must take Math HL instead

### ABF IB Diploma Pathways

**Option 1: IB History HL + Honors Biology**

- IB Science needed: IB Environmental Systems SL (12th grade) or IB Computer Science SL (11th or 12th)
- 11th: TOK, choice of IB Science or optional AP, period 9 for prereqs
- 12th: IB Math HL or SL (in lieu of LADE or Multivariable Calc), choice of IB Science or optional AP
- HL already met with 3 HL courses; optionally HL in World Language OR Math (not both)

**Option 2: IB History HL + IB Biology SL**

- 11th: TOK, optional AP elective, period 9 optional tri electives
- 12th: IB Math HL or SL, optional AP elective
- Same HL situation as Option 1

**Option 3: IB History SL + IB Biology SL**

- 11th: History SL in lieu of optional AP, TOK, optional 2-day elective
- 12th: IB Math HL or SL, up to 2 optional AP electives
- HL REQUIRED: Must take HL in World Language OR Math (or both)
- If Mandarin → must take Math HL; Math HL typically requires Adv Math Analysis II or higher

**Option 4: IB History SL + Honors Biology**

- 11th: History SL in lieu of optional AP, TOK, optional 2-day elective, period 9 for prereqs
- 12th: IB Math HL or SL, IB Science (Env Systems SL or CS SL), optional AP
- Same HL requirement as Option 3

### ACAHA IB Diploma Pathways

IB Business SL already in core. Need additionally: IB Science, History, World Language, Math IB courses.

**Option 1: IB History HL + Honors Biology**

- IB Science: Env Systems SL or CS SL (11th or 12th)
- HL: History + English already; need one more HL → World Language or Math HL

**Option 2: IB History HL + IB Biology SL**

- Optional AP elective in 11th; same HL requirement

**Option 3: IB History SL + IB Biology SL**

- Must take HL in BOTH World Language AND Math
- Not available if World Language is Mandarin
- Math HL requires Adv Math Analysis II or higher

**Option 4: IB History SL + Honors Biology**

- IB Science in 12th (Env Systems or CS)
- Same HL requirement as Option 3 (both World Language and Math HL)

### AEDT / ATCS / AVPA IB Diploma Pathways

Can take IB Biology SL in place of Honors Biology (opens more diploma pathways). Three options:

**Option 1: IB Biology SL + IB History HL**

- IB Elective needed: Env Systems SL (12th only), CS SL, or Business SL
- HL: History + English → need one more (World Language or Math)
- 11th: IB Biology SL instead of Honors Biology, TOK, choice of IB Elective or AP

**Option 2: IB Biology SL + IB History SL**

- Must take HL in BOTH World Language AND Math
- Mandarin students ineligible for this option
- Moves most elective AP/IB courses to 12th grade; optional 2-day elective in 11th

**Option 3: Honors Biology + IB History HL**

- Must take a 4-day IB Elective or IB Science in both 11th and 12th grade
- Options: IB Business SL, IB Env Systems SL (12th only), IB CS SL
- HL: History + English → need one more (World Language or Math)

### AMST and AAST IB Diploma Pathways

Need: IB Science + IB Elective + HL courses in History/World Language/Math + TOK

- 11th: TOK, IB elective or IB science (IB Biology not available to AMST)
- 12th: IB Math HL or SL, another IB elective or science
- Must take HL in World Language or Math (or both)
- IB Biology not available in 12th grade

**Note on Analysis II students and IB Diploma:** ABF and ACAHA students in Math Analysis II as 11th graders can still attain the diploma — in 12th grade, they use their "Optional AP" slot to take AP Calculus AB or Honors Calculus in addition to IB Math SL. ## 11. HISTORY OPTIONS

### 11th Grade History Options

| Course                           | Type                   | Days/Week           | Duration | IB Exam                |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------- | -------- | ---------------------- |
| 20th Century American History I  | Honors, non-IB         | 3                   | 1 year   | No                     |
| IB 20th Century World History SL | IB Standard Level      | 5                   | 1 year   | Yes, end of 11th grade |
| IB History of the Americas I HL  | IB Higher Level Year 1 | 3 (11th) / 4 (12th) | 2 years  | Yes, end of 12th grade |

**20th Century American History I:** Post-Civil War Reconstruction through Cold War. Primary/secondary sources, Socratic seminars, debates, document analysis, formal essays.

**IB History SL (20th Century World History):** One year, comparative and multi-perspective. Topics: WWI (causes/practices/effects), Interwar Years, Great Depression, WWII, Cold War, Late 20th century. Independent research paper (2,300 words), timed essay assessments, debates. Meets 5 days/week.

**IB History HL (History of the Americas):** Two-year course beginning in 11th grade. Year 1: Latin America and North America (revolutions, Great Depression, race/gender/class, global connections). Year 2: Advanced treatment of SL topics. 3 days/week in 11th, 4 days/week in 12th. IB exam at end of 12th grade (3-part exam/essay). Independent research paper (2,300 words).

### 12th Grade History

- IB History HL students automatically continue to Year II
- 20th Century American History II (optional, for students who took year I)
- AP Government (available to all students)
- History not required for other 12th graders

## 12. ENGLISH / LITERATURE OPTIONS

Both 11th grade options meet **4 days per week**. Your 11th grade choice determines your 12th grade English course.

### IB Literature and Language HL

- **Primary focus:** How authorial choice, language, culture, and context impact meaning in BOTH literary AND non-literary texts (nonfiction, film, social media, advertising, etc.)
- **11th grade texts often include:** My Brilliant Friend (Elena Ferrante), The World's Wife (Carol Ann Duffy), selected contemporary poetry, Hamlet (Shakespeare), student-choice graphic memoirs, various non-literary texts (films, essays, social media, speeches, music, ads)
- **Workload:** 3,000 words formal writing/trimester, summer assignments
- **IB Assessments:** 1 analytical essay, 1 oral presentation (11th grade), 2 two-hour essay exams in May of senior year
- **12th Grade continuation:** IB Language and Literature HL Year II

### IB Literature HL

- **Primary focus:** Literary works of various genres — mostly 20th century and contemporary (fiction, poetry, plays, creative nonfiction). Emphasis on close reading, comparative analysis, applying texts to global issues
- **11th grade texts often include:** Oedipus the King and Antigone (Sophocles), The Cherry Orchard (Chekhov), Small Things Like These (Claire Keegan), Remains of the Day (Kazuo Ishiguro), The God of Small Things (Arundhati Roy), The House of the Spirits (Isabel Allende)
- **Workload:** 6–7 books' worth of reading per year, 3,000 words formal writing/trimester, summer assignments
- **IB Assessments:** Same as above (1 analytical essay, 1 oral presentation, 2 two-hour exams senior year)
- **12th Grade continuation:** IB Literature HL Year II

## 18. ART CREDIT REQUIREMENT

- **Graduation requirement:** 6 art credits total
- **Already earned:** 3 credits from mandatory 9th grade art class
- **Still needed:** 3 more credits (= 3 one-trimester art electives OR 1 full-year art course)
- **How to identify art courses:** Elective catalog marks art-eligible courses with "YES" in the Art column
- **Ways to earn art credits:**
  - Period 9 trimester electives (in off-season)
  - Projects
  - Lunch Choir
  - Year-long two-day-per-week art elective

**Examples of art-eligible trimester electives:** Ceramics, Creative Art Workshop, Culinary Design (Baking/Western Cuisine), Design for 3D Printing, Digital Photography, History of Rock Music, Interactive Design, Intro to Photoshop, Musical Theater, Pit Orchestra, Public Speaking, Video Production, Yoga, Costume Design, Dance Fitness (Zumba), Haunted Mansion, One Act Play, Orchestra, Concert Band, Concert Choir, Stage Management, Voice Over and Podcasting, Intro Architecture, Drawing (full year), Pre AP Studio Art (full year), Fashion Design and Sewing (full year), Screenwriting (full year)

## 19. KEY RULES & CONSTRAINTS

### Scheduling Rules

- Students **cannot request more classes than can fit** in their schedule
- 12th graders are scheduled before 11th graders, who are before 10th graders, etc.
- Core classes are scheduled before 3/4-day electives, which are scheduled before 2-day electives
- Rising 11th grade requests are for **periods 1–8 only** — period 9 is handled separately in June
- Students may submit **one alternate** elective choice in case the primary doesn't fit

### Course-Specific Restrictions

**Bloomberg:**

- Full-year Bloomberg Markets_Trading 1-3 (periods 1–8) and trimester Bloomberg ~Bloomberg 1: Equities cover overlapping content. Do NOT take both.

**SQL / MERN Stack:**

- ~Structured Query Language (SQL) trimester elective and MERN Stack Web Apps full-year elective cover overlapping content. Do NOT take both.

**Design for 3D Printing:**

- Not a repeatable elective.

**Ceramics:**

- Students who have previously taken Ceramics may only request it during Add/Drop, not during CRP.

**Culinary Design:**

- Students who have previously taken Culinary Design (Baking Fundamentals or 9th grade Culinary) may only request it during Add/Drop.

**Calculus I and PE/2-day electives:**

- Due to scheduling, Calculus I (3 days/week) cannot be paired with a 2-day elective or PE. Adjacent periods remain open except if filled by research.

**IB History HL:**

- A two-year commitment. Students who choose it in 11th grade are enrolled in Year II in 12th grade.

**IB World Language HL:**

- A two-year commitment. Students in HL must continue to Year II in 12th grade.

**IB Math:**

- Choosing IB Math HL replaces LADE or Multivariable Calculus in 12th grade.

**AP/IB Exam requirement:**

- All students in AP or IB courses MUST sit for the AP/IB exams. Exam fees are required.

**Foundations of Computer Science:**

- Non-ATCS students must complete the full 3-trimester FoCS sequence as a prerequisite for AP CS, IB CS, and many other CS electives.
- Can be taken as: full-year two-day elective (recommended) OR three separate trimester electives (FoCS-1 in T1, FoCS-2 in T2, FoCS-3 in T3)
- If doing period 9 sequence and planning to play a sport or do theatre in period 9, you won't be able to complete the sequence — take the full-year version instead.

**Intro to Python prerequisites:**

- ATCS: May take in Trimester 2 of 9th grade or later
- Non-ATCS: Must have completed at least 2 trimesters of the FoCS sequence

**Machine Learning prerequisites:**

- ATCS: 10th grade or higher
- Non-ATCS: Must have completed the full 3-trimester FoCS sequence

**Research in Cell Biology:**

- Not available to AMST students (they do not need it for research)
- Grade restriction: 9th and 10th grade only
- Prerequisite: Intro to STEM Research (for students who haven't completed core bio yet)

**Intro to Makerspace:**

- Not available to AEDT students (they take a similar course as part of their core)

**Economics of Euro Zone:**

- Limited to ABF and ACAHA students; grades 9–10 only

**Fed Challenge I:**

- By application only; ABF students only; grades 11–12

**Foundations of Environmental Systems (T3):**

- By application only; grade 11 only
- Mandatory prerequisite for students wishing to take IB Environmental Systems and Societies in 12th grade

**Intro to C Programming:**

- Not available to ATCS 11th graders (covered in their core)
- Equivalent to T2 of full-year Intro Systems Programming

**Sockets and Threading (T3):**

- Part of full-year Intro Systems Programming. Do NOT take if enrolled in that course.

**Assembly Language Basics (T1):**

- Similar content to Intro Systems Programming. Do NOT take if you have already taken that course.

**Processors I & II:**

- Must be taken in sequence (I before II)
- Prereq or coreq: AP Computer Science
- Processors I: T2; Processors II: T3

**Intro to Web App Development (T3):**

- All non-ATCS students (except ATCS 10th graders) must have completed SQL first

**Topics in Hematology/Oncology:**

- By application only; 20+ year Expert Series elective
- All grades eligible

**History of Rock Music:**

- Students may take in any combination of trimesters (T1 = 1950s, T2 = early 1960s, T3 = late 1960s–1970s)

**Interactive Design sequence:**

- Level I → Level II (must have taken Level I) → Level III (must have taken Level II)
- Advanced Topics I → II → III (each requires the previous)

### Diploma-Specific Constraints

- If pursuing IB Diploma, TOK (Theory of Knowledge) must be taken in BOTH 11th and 12th grades
- TOK can be taken at period 9 to free up a 2-day elective slot during periods 1–8
- IB Environmental Systems SL is only available in 12th grade for some pathways (check your specific academy pathway)
- IB Biology SL is NOT available in 12th grade
